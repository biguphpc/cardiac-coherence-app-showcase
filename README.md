## What is the Coherence App
Coherence is a powerful science-backed breathing app designed to synchronize your heart and mind through cardiac coherence breathing.

Based on over 30 years of research, this simple exercise helps you relax from stress, sharpen mental focus and achieve physiological balance in a few minutes.

🌱 Completely free, works offline, no ads or account needed.

**features:**
- Guided Cardiac Coherence Breathing Exercises
- Beautiful, Minimalist Design for a calming experience
- Personalized Reminders to Help You Stay Consistent
- Progress Tracking and Session History to Monitor Your Journey
- Real-Time Visual Feedback to Enhance Focus and Relaxation
- Customizable Session Duration
- Scientifically Backed Techniques
- Offline Functionality for Anytime, Anywhere Access
- Available on iOS and Android, phone, tablets, computers and on the Web
